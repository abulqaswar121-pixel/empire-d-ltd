Our story

Built from the classroom outwards.

{{company.name}} began with a straightforward observation: the gap in Nigerian education is rarely about ambition — it is about access. Access to good teaching, to the right learning materials, and to the equipment that makes modern learning possible.

We started out supporting schools and training providers with materials and learning support. As demand grew, so did the request around it: can you deliver it too? and can you source the rest of what we need? Today, education remains our lead business, with logistics and general procurement operating alongside it as services in their own right.

The result is a company that can run a programme end to end — from the content and the equipment, to the delivery at your gate, with one team answering for all of it.
